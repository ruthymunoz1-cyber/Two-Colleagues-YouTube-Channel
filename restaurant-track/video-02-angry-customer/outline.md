# Video 2 — Outline

**Working title:** When the Customer Is Angry — De-escalation Phrases That Work
**Status:** OUTLINE — full script to be drafted (launch batch, handoff v2)
**Characters:** Maria (server) · James (manager — role-plays the angry customer)

## Concept

Maria faces her first genuinely angry customer (wrong order arrived, customer is loud). Panic response vs. professional response. James teaches the de-escalation ladder: acknowledge → apologize once → fix it → escalate if needed. Payoff scene: Maria calmly handles the same angry customer from the cold open.

## Phrase Bank (draft — 8 phrases, whiteboard recap only)

1. "I'm so sorry about that."
2. "You're right — that's not what you ordered."
3. "Let me fix that for you right away."
4. "Thank you for your patience."
5. "I completely understand."
6. "Would you like me to get my manager?"
7. "This one is on us." *(with manager approval — teach the escalation rule)*
8. "Is there anything else I can make right?"

## Teaching beats

1. **Skill 1 — Don't match their energy:** lower voice, slower pace (the counter-instinct move)
2. **Skill 2 — Acknowledge before you explain:** "You're right" disarms; excuses escalate
3. **Skill 3 — Fix or escalate:** what a server can offer vs. when to hand off to the manager — and how to hand off without it feeling like defeat
4. **Emotional core (this episode carries the channel promise hardest):** being yelled at in your second language feels worse — James names this directly and normalizes it

## Required structure (same as Video 1)

Cold open (angry customer, freeze frame) → training frame → 3 skills with role-play → payoff replay → whiteboard recap quiz (Q → 3s pause → A) → CTA (next episode tease: Kitchen English) → 5-scene Shorts promo
