## Concept

End of Maria's shift; the closer arrives. A bad handover ("table 9 is… whatever, you'll figure it out") vs. a professional one. Handover is where B1–B2 workers silently lose credibility — you must *summarize* and *prioritize* in English, not just react. This is the most advanced episode of the launch batch: it teaches structured speaking.

## Teaching beats

1. **Skill 1 — The handover order:** urgent → in-progress → good-to-know (priority structure, not a memory dump)
2. **Skill 2 — One sentence per table:** the summarizing skill (status + what's next)
3. **Skill 3 — Close the loop:** confirm the other person has no questions before leaving
4. **Launch-batch finale touches:** callbacks to all four earlier episodes (the fast customer, the angry customer, kitchen shorthand, the covered shift) — rewards binge-watching the launch batch and strings the 5 videos into a story
