## Concept

The kitchen is its own language: rapid-fire callouts, shorthand, and slang that no textbook teaches ("86 the salmon!", "Behind!", "Fire table 12", "On the fly"). Maria keeps freezing at the pass. James decodes kitchen shorthand — this is a *listening comprehension* episode more than a speaking one.

## Phrase Bank (draft — kitchen callouts + server responses, whiteboard recap only)

1. "86" = we're out of it → response: "Got it — 86 salmon."
2. "Behind!" / "Corner!" = safety callouts → response: repeat it back as you move
3. "On the fly" = urgent, make it fast
4. "Fire table 12" = start cooking that table's order now
5. "Running food" = carrying plates out
6. "All day" = the total count of one dish across all tickets
7. "Heard!" = the one-word confirmation the kitchen expects
8. "How long on table 8?" = the server's status-check question

## Teaching beats

1. **Skill 1 — The confirmation habit:** always answer a callout ("Heard!") — silence reads as not listening
2. **Skill 2 — Decode the shorthand:** the 6 callouts you'll hear every single shift
3. **Skill 3 — Ask for status without annoying the kitchen:** "How long on…?" timing and tone
4. **Differentiator note:** this worker-side kitchen content is exactly the vidIQ-identified gap — zero competition
