## Locked Pairing (English track)

### Maria — Restaurant Server (learner proxy)

The audience sees themselves in Maria: capable, hardworking, newer to English-speaking shifts. She asks the questions the viewer would ask.

| Attribute | Proposal |
|-----------|----------|
| Age | Adult (late 20s) |
| Look | Medium olive skin (typical Latina tone), dark hair in a practical low bun, brown eyes |
| Outfit | Black server apron over a fitted black polo, order pad in apron pocket, name tag "MARIA" |
| Voice direction | Warm, slightly careful pacing — confidence grows across episodes |
| Role in scripts | Learner proxy: asks questions, repeats phrases, makes the realistic small mistakes viewers make |

### James — Restaurant Manager (mentor)

The experienced colleague, not a boss-villain. He models phrases, role-plays customers during training moments, and never talks down to Maria.

| Attribute | Proposal |
|-----------|----------|
| Age | Adult (40s) |
| Look | Light-medium skin, short dark hair with gray at the temples, friendly lined face |
| Outfit | Dark button-down with rolled sleeves, manager lanyard/badge |
| Voice direction | Steady, encouraging, naturally faster speech — he IS the "fast speaker" the viewer must learn to understand |
| Role in scripts | Mentor: models phrases at natural speed, then slows down; plays the customer in role-play segments |

**Why this pairing:** Maria is the top server name in the vidIQ naming table; James is the top manager name. Server-as-learner + manager-as-mentor keeps every episode a genuine *two colleagues* conversation (matching the channel name) — the "customer" can be role-played by James in training scenes, which also keeps AI generation to two consistent characters instead of introducing a third face every episode.
