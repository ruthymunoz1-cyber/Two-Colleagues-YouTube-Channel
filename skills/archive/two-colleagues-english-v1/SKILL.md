## 🎨 VISUAL STYLE — STANDARD

Apply this style block to EVERY scene prompt unless a scene-specific override is noted.

```
Style: Simple soft cartoon illustration, clean 3D digital art, warm beige
and pastel colors, cozy office atmosphere, minimal background details,
cute modern character design, smooth clean shading, soft drop shadows,
lifestyle illustration aesthetic, polished educational animation look.
```

### Style Rules
- ✅ ALWAYS: 3D cartoon illustration style
- ✅ ALWAYS: Warm beige, cream, and soft pastel tones
- ✅ ALWAYS: Cozy minimal office or everyday environments
- ✅ ALWAYS: Consistent character appearance in EVERY scene
- ❌ NEVER: Realistic or photographic style
- ❌ NEVER: Complex camera movements or cinematic language
- ❌ NEVER: Dramatic lighting (no golden hour, neon, fog)
- ❌ NEVER: Different character appearance between scenes

### Special Environment Overrides
- **Blue Conference Room** (Scenes 13–14, Video 1): Use soft blue accent tones
  in background; blue-tinted glass door visible. Style note:
  `"Simple soft cartoon illustration, clean 3D digital art, soft blue accent tones..."`

---

## 📝 DIALOG FORMAT — STANDARD

Every scene uses two dialog lines:

- **Dialog 1** = Normal speaking speed (complete natural sentence)
- **Dialog 2** = Slow, broken syllables for learners (e.g., `"Good... morn-ing!"`)

### Rules
- A1–A2 English ONLY — no idioms, no slang, no complex grammar
- Short, natural, real-life workplace sentences
- Dialog 2 must break every word into syllables with `...` between each word/syllable

## 📋 PRODUCTION STANDARDS

### YouTube Title Formula
- SEO-optimized, A1–A2 focused
- Include "English" + real-life situation + beginner signal
- Examples: "Learn English at the Office", "English at the Café", "Daily English at Home"

### Shorts Promo Formula (per video)
- 5 scenes, vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds
- Scene 1: Hook with bold text overlay (0–5 sec)
- Scenes 2–4: 3 best dialog samples with text overlay
- Scene 5: Call to action pointing to full video

### Document Naming Convention
`RAMP_[Topic]_VideoScript.docx`

### Batch Prompt Delivery
- Generate ALL dialogs first before any scene prompts
- Deliver scene prompts in batches of 5
- User types YES / NEXT / CONTINUE to advance
